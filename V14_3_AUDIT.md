# FahadDev v14.3 Funnel Audit

## Primary change
The homepage was shortened and reorganized around client buying logic rather than developer-process repetition.

## Funnel sequence
Hero → Launch benefits → Pain → Implementation → Proof → Reviews → FAQs → Contact CTA.

## Buyer questions now answered early
- What will I actually receive?
- Will it work properly on mobile?
- Can I manage content or operations after launch?
- Can leads, data, auth or payments be connected?
- Is SEO/performance/deployment part of the job?
- What business problem is this solving?
- Is there proof of implementation?
- How do I start without writing a technical specification?

## Removed homepage repetition
- generic proof ribbon
- service category wall already duplicated by the interactive showcase
- second benefits section
- value/outcome band
- technology logo wall
- partner logo block
- risk-reduction cards
- generic homepage process story

The detailed Services and Process pages are retained instead of duplicating their content on Home.

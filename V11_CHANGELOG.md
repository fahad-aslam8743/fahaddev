# FahadDev v11 changelog

## Direction

The site was intentionally moved away from a static/corporate brochure feel toward a live independent-builder experience. The funnel, CMS, lead inbox, review approval flow, SEO routes and Supabase architecture from v10 remain intact.

## Interaction system

1. Reactive ambient pointer light on public pages.
2. Scroll progress indicator.
3. Scroll-aware glass navigation.
4. Interactive 3D hero systems with scanner, bar motion and status cues.
5. Kinetic capability rails throughout major page heroes.
6. Interactive outcome selector on the homepage.
7. Pointer tilt + glare on work cards.
8. Richer reveal, card and FAQ transitions.
9. Reduced-motion accessibility fallback.

## Copy tone

The main page heroes were made more direct:

- Work: focuses on what was built and why.
- Services: starts from what is not working.
- Process: emphasizes no black box.
- About: emphasizes one builder and direct execution.
- Contact: starts with "Got something that should work better?"

## Backend/admin

No database or admin workflow change in v11. v10 lead inbox, project CMS, image upload and moderated reviews are preserved.
